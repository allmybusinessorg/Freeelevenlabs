## 🚀 Overview

**Creative Audio AI** is a revolutionary, open-source desktop application engineered to provide unlimited, 100% offline AI voice cloning and continuous speech generation for Windows 10 & 11 (64-bit). 

Powered by OpenBMB's state-of-the-art **VoxCPM2 2-Billion parameter engine**, Creative Audio AI allows content creators, YouTubers, audio engineers, and developers to generate 48kHz studio-quality voiceovers locally on their own PC with **zero monthly subscriptions**, **zero character caps**, and **zero data tracking**.

## 🌟 Key Capabilities

- 🎭 **Zero-Shot Voice Cloning**: Clone any human voice with continuous acoustic pitch fidelity using just a 3-second reference audio sample.
- 🎨 **Controllable Voice Design**: Describe voice emotion, accent, age, pitch, and speech tempo in plain natural text.
- ⚡ **Local PyTorch CUDA Acceleration**: Generates 10 seconds of audio in 1 to 3 seconds on consumer NVIDIA GeForce RTX GPUs (RTX 3050, 3060, 4070+).
- 🔒 **100% Data Privacy**: Your voice recordings, text prompts, and generated audio files never leave your computer.
